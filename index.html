Component Anatomy & Tolerances

Explore The Mechanical Engineering

Engineered for extreme RPMs, heavy torque dynamics, and long maintenance lifecycles.

Precision Bearing Anatomy

  • Inner/Outer Rings: GCr15 High Carbon Chrome Steel with vacuum degassing.
  • Rolling Elements: Grade 10 micro-polished steel balls (V2/V3 vibration).
  • Sealing: Synthetic NBR rubber contact lip seal (2RS) or zinc steel shield (2Z).

Timing Belt Construction

  • Tensile Cord: Continuously wound helically twisted fiberglass cords.
  • Teeth Facing: Wear-resistant nylon fabric with anti-friction coating.
  • Elastomer Body: High-grade chloroprene compound resisting oil & heat to 100°C.

Linear Guide Rails & Blocks

  • 4-Way Equal Load: 45-degree contact angle absorbs equal loads in all 4 axes.
  • Self-Aligning: Absorbs installation surface deviations for whisper-quiet CNC travel.
  • Dust Sealing: Dual end seals and bottom scrapers keep internal raceways clear.
